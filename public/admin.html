ORGANIZER CHEATSHEET

How to Run the Bingo Icebreaker

Quick 4-step walkthrough for hosting a smooth, fun session.

📺

1. Project the QR Code on Stage

Click "📺 Projector Mode & QR" in the top navigation. Project the screen on the auditorium projector. Students point their camera to open the game—no app downloads required.

🎲

2. Zero-Friction Enrollment & Random Cards

Students only enter their name (no passwords). Each player receives a genuinely unique, randomized 5x5 card drawn from your active task pool with a central Free Space.

✍️

3. Mingling & Signature Rules

To check a square, students must type the name of the person they met. Anti-Cheat: The same person can sign at most 2 squares per card, and max 8 times across the entire event.

🏆

4. Live Inspection & Winner Verification

When a student achieves BINGO (5 lines), a golden 🏆 BINGO badge appears next to their name in your table. Click "🔍 Inspect" to view their exact board and verify who signed their squares before awarding prizes!
