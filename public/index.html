How to Play Human Bingo

Break the ice, find classmates & achieve 5 lines!

🤝
1. Mingle & Search
Walk around and strike up conversations to find students matching your card's prompts (e.g. "Uses Linux" or "From a different branch").
✍️
2. Get Their Name
Tap the matching square, type their name, and confirm. Rule: Each person can sign at most 2 squares on your card!
🏆
3. Complete 5 Lines & Yell BINGO!
Each horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line lights up a letter in B-I-N-G-O. Complete 5 lines to claim your prize from the organizers!
